  - **A readable canvas.** An opened example shows ratings and results on the cards; the automatic fit stops at 75 % and keeps the selected card in view; a selected or newly added card is always brought into view; scrolling moves the view and Ctrl/⌘ + scroll or a pinch zooms (the wheel used to zoom 7 % per event whatever its size); two fingers move and zoom on a touch screen; below 100 % the selected card's actions follow it at a fixed size.

  - **What just happened is said.** Deleting a card shows *Poistettu: …* with *Kumoa*; a confirming message leaves by itself after seven seconds.
  - **No small print.** Outside the cards of the canvas no text is under 12 px (labels, notes, menu shortcuts and table details were 9–11 px).
  - **Less in the toolbar rows.** The document's type (Argumentointi / Päätös) moved from the row of view switches into the Tiedosto menu and is shown beside the title; language and theme sit behind a ⚙ button; a decision's toolbar fits one row at 1440 px; on a phone the header takes five rows (about 220 px) instead of seven (about 350 px), and the inspector is a sheet with a handle and three heights: lowered to give the tree the screen, half with the claim and its credence slider beside the selected card, or nearly full.
  - **First use.** The guided practice has nine steps: it now also has the objection rated and the main claim's result read. *Uusi työ* highlights the blank argument tree instead of the AI route, and a new document opens with its title ready to be written. - Owner request 2026-11-18 (WG-031): the assessment round goes from the leaves to the root under the two new calculation methods and shows, for a claim with reasons, your earlier rating, what your own ratings of the reasons make of the figure on the slider, and the weightiest reasons; the group's computed result only when asked for. - Owner decisions 2026-11-18 (WG-029), after a review of the calculation model on the example trees and on 4 500 synthetic trees: two calculation methods. **Laskenta koko puusta** (`anchored-reasons-v1`), the default for new documents: a claim's rating is the anchor its reasons move (it weighs as one fully credible ±2 reason), an unrated claim shows the net of its reasons and passes it upward only when positive, options and the main claim have no ±100 cap (+50, +66.7, +75), and adding a reason under a rated claim can no longer move the result the wrong way. **Laskenta suorista perusteluista** (`direct-reasons-v1`): a rated claim passes its rating upward as it is; what its reasons give is shown beside it. Under both, a rated claim's result is a credence shown as a percentage, never on the −100…+100 scale. The five earlier methods are under *Vanhat* in the select, and a line under the select describes the selected method. Existing documents keep their method; the generated examples now carry the new default. Both guides gain a paragraph on tidying the tree before it is rated (the author is responsible for not repeating a reason under one node).

The prototype's stylesheet is now `src/app/styles.css` (eight themes). `src/domain` gains `layout.ts`, `navigation.ts`, `lens.ts`, `table.ts` and `ai/` (ai-draft v2, contribution v2 and bundle v1, starter v1, import preflight), each a line-for-line port with parity tests that load the frozen prototype modules; `src/lib` gains `workspaceMeta.ts`, `library.ts` (IndexedDB) and `export/` (PNG/SVG/PDF). View state uses the prototype's `witt-view-v1`, `witt-zones-v1`, `witt-appearance-v1`, `witt-assessor-v1:<id>`, `witt-review-view-v1:<id>`, `witt-assessment-pass-v2:<id>:<actor>` and `witt-keybindings-v1` shapes; base themes are document data, skins are per browser. Browser walks start from the home screen and use the prototype's element ids.

### Changed
- WG-021: the locale catalog is split. `src/lib/i18n/catalog.ts` carries the languages, the typed key list and Finnish; `src/lib/i18n/messages/<lang>.ts` are generated per language and loaded on demand through `loaders.ts` (one chunk each); `translate(messages, key, params)` takes the loaded map and falls back to Finnish. The main bundle fell from 770 kB to 383 kB (119 kB gzip). The tests load every language and check completeness and placeholder parity per module; a browser walk switches to English and back.
