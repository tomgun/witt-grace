## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Owner decisions 2026-11-09: a node can have its own type name (*Oma tyyppinimi*, e.g. Kriteeri, Tavoite, Kandidaatti) shown on the card instead of the built-in tag and passed on to the next sibling option or value; the `,` key adds a value judgment under the selected card (rebindable); two synthetic recruitment examples (one candidate against criteria, three candidates on the same criteria) generated by `scripts/build-recruitment-examples.ts`. New document field `typeLabel`; labels in nine languages (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15).
- G-02 in `GRACE_AND_DATA_MODEL.md`, the user guides and the in-app help (`helpImportance`, nine languages) explain the marginal effect Δ shown by *Korosta painavimmat*: what it answers, how it is computed, two worked examples, why only some cards show it. `tests/unit/marginal-effects.test.ts` pins the examples. WG-028 records the owner's question about an unrated note node and the impact wording.
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: three themes, per browser like the other skins: ▮ Terminaali (green phosphor terminal), # ASCII (cards and tree edges drawn from characters; `src/app/AsciiEdge.tsx`) and ✎ Sarjakuva (xkcd-like hand-drawn look with the bundled Comic Neue font, SIL OFL 1.1, `src/app/fonts/`). Their styles live in `src/app/themes-added.css`, after the untouched prototype stylesheet. Labels in nine languages (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15).

### Changed
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: a claim's result comes from its reasons; its own credence is a felt sense that does not count (new scoring version `evidence-v4-reasons`, default for new documents; name provisional). The calculation method is switchable per document in Arviointi → Laskentatapa; the prototype's `evidence-v3-links` stays available and pins the golden fixtures. The recruitment examples are regenerated under v4. Labels in nine languages.
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: in a decision, a reason directly under an option is rated as its *significance for the option* (direction, strength and how much it matters to the assessor in one number): new wording on the inspector slider, its kicker and the assessment round, in nine languages; deeper reasons keep *influence on the parent*. Scoring unchanged (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15).
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: an unassessed inspector slider is greyed and reads "—" instead of showing the document's starting value as if it counted; pressing or moving it saves it, also at the shown value, and saving one axis also saves the other as shown when the person has none yet, as one undo step (`review.personal`). New hint `profileUnsetHint` in nine languages (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15).
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: the separate card layers Uskottavuusarvio and Vaikutuspaino are removed (they repeated *Arviot tiiviisti* 75%/+5, which the Kaikki tiedot preset now shows), and so is *Raahaa solmua myös tekstistä*: cards always drag by their text too (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15).
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: cards have a × delete button at the right edge (apart from + − ▾); a leaf is deleted without a confirm by × or Del, a subtree still asks; the deleted cards fade out for a second so an accidental delete is seen, and undo restores them (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15). New catalog key `deleteNode` in nine languages.
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: a ? button in the top bar opens the help and keyboard shortcuts (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15); on phones the language and theme selects narrow to keep the row on screen.
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: undo ↶ and redo ↷ buttons are always visible in the top bar, left of the panel toggles; disabled when there is nothing to undo or redo (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15).
- Owner decision 2026-11-09: the Bitberg theme is now labelled “◆ Yövärit” (en “◆ Night colours”, all nine languages) with a glyph like the other themes; the theme id `bitberg` and stored preferences are unchanged (`UI_INVENTORY.md` S-15).

### Changed
- WG-023 (parity programme, parts 1–2): after the owner compared the first rebuild with the prototype, the prototype's visible UI became the contract (`docs/spec/UI_INVENTORY.md`, every region, control, dialog, layer, stage, persisted key and format). The app was rebuilt against it: home screen; top bar with home and about; toolbar with the Kartoita/Punnitse/Päätösäänestys stages, the actor select with "+ Lisää paikallinen käyttäjä…", the five menus item for item and the perspective banner; left panel with outline, the `Yhteydet ja perustelut` pane and the add block; canvas cards with inline `+ − * ▦ ▾` buttons, author marks, chips per layer, the context menu, inline title editing, drag (also from the title), three orientations with the prototype's layout maths, compact and depth projections, branch focus, secondary-link edges with arrow markers, zoom/pan/fit, the quick bar in "Vain puu"; the `Näkymäasetukset` dock; the inspector section by section (library-reason inspector, concept and locked boxes, relation and parent selects, the "Arvioinnin mukaan" line, the Punnitse hint, own sliders, comparison, score hero with breakdown, advanced details with sources and overlap note, concept uses, legacy links, comments, options ranking); the assessment round, review lens, vote, people, help (with customisable shortcuts), links, examples, replace, library, visual export dialogs and the guided tutorial; the table presentation with the mobile list. The prototype's stylesheet is now `src/app/styles.css` (eight themes). `src/domain` gains `layout.ts`, `navigation.ts`, `lens.ts`, `table.ts` and `ai/` (ai-draft v2, contribution v2 and bundle v1, starter v1, import preflight), each a line-for-line port with parity tests that load the frozen prototype modules; `src/lib` gains `workspaceMeta.ts`, `library.ts` (IndexedDB) and `export/` (PNG/SVG/PDF). View state uses the prototype's `witt-view-v1`, `witt-zones-v1`, `witt-appearance-v1`, `witt-assessor-v1:<id>`, `witt-review-view-v1:<id>`, `witt-assessment-pass-v2:<id>:<actor>` and `witt-keybindings-v1` shapes; base themes are document data, skins are per browser. Browser walks start from the home screen and use the prototype's element ids.

### Changed
- WG-021: the locale catalog is split. `src/lib/i18n/catalog.ts` carries the languages, the typed key list and Finnish; `src/lib/i18n/messages/<lang>.ts` are generated per language and loaded on demand through `loaders.ts` (one chunk each); `translate(messages, key, params)` takes the loaded map and falls back to Finnish. The main bundle fell from 770 kB to 383 kB (119 kB gzip). The tests load every language and check completeness and placeholder parity per module; a browser walk switches to English and back.

### Added
- WG-012: repeated reasons. A claim's inspector offers "Liitä myös muihin solmuihin…": a target picker (only targets the domain accepts) creates the concept and a new use under the target, selected and visible at once, with its own ratings. A use shows the concept box (manage in the shelf, detach keeping its ratings) and a badge on its card. The left panel's second tab is the shelf: create a card (`Luo kirjastoon`, a blank title becomes "Uusi perustelu"), see every concept with its uses and each use's own `75%/+5`, open a use's reviews, attach a card to the selected node. `canReuseClaimAt` joins the domain. `tests/e2e/reuse-walk.spec.ts` rates the same reason 70 %/+4 under one option and 90 %/−3 under another.
- WG-015 (table half): the argument table beside the tree — every claim in logical order with its direct relation, context, this person's credence and influence and the balance; search and relation filter; a tree/table switch in the toolbar. `index.html` carries a Content-Security-Policy (scripts and connections from the page's own origin only, no inline script, no framing).
- WG-011: decision mode in the app. The decision root's inspector shows the options overview (feasibility badge from gates, balance, ballot preference, ordered as the domain ranks them: feasibility, completeness, balance). The Arviointi menu opens the people dialog (names, vote shares, the pooled-weighting choice equal/shares/structural, switch, remove) and, in decision mode, the ballot dialog: weights frozen when a round opens, interim results hidden until the round closes, per-person 0–100 sliders with save and retract, the owner closes the round, closed rounds archive with their results. `tests/e2e/decision-walk.spec.ts` walks two options, a review, a second person and a full round.
- WG-007: the new app. React 19 + Vite + TypeScript (ADR A005): `src/app/store.ts` holds the document and dispatches serializable commands with undo/redo and autosave; the shell has the prototype's zones — top bar (title, `Alt+1..4` pane toggles, language, theme incl. Bitberg), toolbar (Argumentointi/Päätös, person, viewpoint, File/Edit/View/Help), outline, pannable and zoomable canvas with draggable cards, edges, balance and `75%/+5` pairs, inspector (title, details, relation, parent, semantic type, gate status, score explanation with contributions, personal review sliders, comments), bottom zone, `Alt+V` dock, footer with the prototype link. Keyboard: `+`/`−` add, `Del`, `F2`, `Ctrl/Cmd+Z`, canvas zoom and pan. JSON export and open with the replacement confirmation that names the current work. Playwright (`npm run e2e`): seven keyboard-first walks at desktop and mobile widths. CI gains a `browser` job; the Pages workflow publishes the app at `/` and the prototype at `/proto/`.
- WG-006: the nine-language catalog. `locales/messages.psv` is the prototype's table (866 keys); `npm run i18n` (`scripts/generate-locales.ts`) renders the typed `src/lib/i18n/catalog.ts` (`MessageKey`, `Language`); `src/lib/i18n/i18n.ts` gives `translate(language, key, params)` and a bound `translator`. Tests pin that the committed catalog equals the rendered table, that every key is non-empty and not raw in all nine languages, that `{placeholders}` agree across languages, and the Finnish glossary words.
- WG-005: `src/domain/serialize.ts` — the `.witt.json` file form (`JSON.stringify(validated, null, 2) + '\n'`), byte-identical to the prototype's export for every example and stable under a second round-trip; the compact storage form; the export filename slug (pinned to the prototype's NFKD quirk).
- WG-004: every document change is a serializable `Command` (43 types) applied by `applyCommand(project, command, { now, actorId })` in `src/domain/commands.ts`: pure, ids supplied by the caller so a peer can replay the same log, the input never mutated, refusals with the prototype's messages, `lastEditedBy` and `createdBy` tagged and the result re-validated like the prototype's mutation wrapper. `src/domain/project.ts` creates blank documents and tidy-tree layouts; `src/domain/views.ts` holds the view-only projections (visible nodes, focus highlighting that respects explicit collapses, option group path, guided review order, value profile, evidence-overlap note). `tests/unit/commands.test.ts` replays 60 edits through the prototype engine and through the commands with identical ids and clock and asserts identical documents after every step.
- WG-003: `src/domain` in TypeScript — the v8 model and closed sets (`model.ts`), the parser as the single trust boundary (`parse.ts`, the prototype's Finnish refusal messages kept), the `evidence-v3-links` engine for the collective viewpoints and per person (`scoring.ts`, with marginal effects), review readers (`reviews.ts`), feasibility (`eligibility.ts`), ballot preferences (`ballots.ts`) and rankings (`ranking.ts`). Pure by construction: no DOM, storage, clock or randomness; imports only inside the folder (ESLint and `tests/unit/import-direction.test.ts`). Tooling: TypeScript 5.9 strict with Node's native type stripping (Node 24; `.nvmrc`), ESLint 9 + typescript-eslint, Prettier, `node --test`. `npm run check` now runs docs, spec lock, lint, typecheck, format and tests.
- WG-002: golden fixtures generated from the frozen prototype engine (`scripts/golden-from-proto.mjs`, `npm run golden`): every v8 example plus 20 hand-built cases (the G-02 table, four levels with sign reversal, a creation stance overridden by a rating, a missing review, a stale fingerprint, a thesis with its own vote, equal vs shares, a zero-share user and an AI profile, structural preview, gates and values, option groups, a reason concept in two contexts, a locked shared reason, a secondary link with a route note, ballot rounds with shares). `tests/golden/golden.test.ts` asserts the new domain reproduces every number exactly: 229 golden assertions green on the first run.
